Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short

Respire collects no personal data. It has no Internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no account. Everything it records stays on your phone.

What Respire accesses

  • The name of the app coming to the foreground, through Android's accessibility service, to show a pause before the apps you chose.
  • If you turn on the Reels or Shorts filter: the technical IDs of on-screen elements, only inside Instagram and YouTube, to detect the short-video player. Text, messages and images are never read.
  • The list of installed apps that have an icon, so you can choose which ones to slow down.

What Respire stores, on your phone only

  • Your settings and the apps you chose.
  • The times of recent pauses and opens (last 24 hours), used for waits and limits.
  • Daily and hourly counts of pauses, opens, times you gave up and Reels or Shorts skipped, kept for up to 60 days.
  • A short diagnostic text listing technical element IDs, if you use the filters.

This data stays in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not included in Android cloud backups.

What Respire shares

Nothing. Without the Internet permission, the app cannot send data. The diagnostic text only leaves the app if you tap Copy and paste it yourself.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Respire, or clear its storage in Settings, Apps, Respire, Storage.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
